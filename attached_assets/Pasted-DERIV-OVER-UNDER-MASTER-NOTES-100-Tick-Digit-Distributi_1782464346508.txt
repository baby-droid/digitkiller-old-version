CHAPTER 3
Reading the Last 100 Ticks

Example

1 = 9%

2 = 7%

3 = 11%

4 = 13%

5 = 12%

6 = 11%

7 = 10%

8 = 9%

9 = 10%

You can total the percentages that would count as wins for a given contract.

Example:

Over 4

5 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 9

12 +11 +10 +9 +10

=52%

This tells you what has happened in the recent sample, not what must happen next.

CHAPTER 21
Observation Zones

You may classify recent 100-tick observations like this:

Winning Digits in Recent Sample	Interpretation
48–52%	Balanced
53–57%	Mild imbalance
58–62%	Strong imbalance
63%+	Extreme recent imbalance

These are descriptive categories, not trading signals.
